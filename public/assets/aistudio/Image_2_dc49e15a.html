Rigorous Execution

8-Stage Sovereign Export Roadmap

From first sample inquiry to destination harbor release: total operational clarity at every milestone.

01

Inquiry & Technical Spec Sheet

Buyer submits commodity parameters (moisture, size calibration, foreign matter tolerance) and target discharge port.

02

Sample Dispatch via Air Courier

DHL/FedEx physical sample dispatched with certified lab COA (Certificate of Analysis) within 48 hours for buyer approval.

03

Proforma Invoice & Contract Sign-off

Final commercial contract executed with exact incoterms, packaging artwork, delivery windows, and arbitration clauses.

04

Order Confirmation & LC / Escrow

Secure payment settlement opened: Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at Sight) from Tier-1 international bank or agreed Advance TT.

05

Farm Quality Check & Sortex Grading

Multi-tier grading at aggregation hubs with computerized color sorting and gravity separation.

06

Custom Packing & Methyl Bromide Fumigation

Sealed export packaging followed by certified phytosanitary gas fumigation compliant with importing nation laws.

07

Customs Clearance & Vessel Loading

Port container stuffing under high-definition photo/video audit at Mundra or JNPT with government shipping bill generation.

08

Bill of Lading & Destination Discharge

Original negotiable shipping documents, Insurance, Health Certificate & COO surrendered through bank for swift clearance.

Trade Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Export Questions

For maritime container shipments, our standard MOQ is 1 Full Container Load (FCL): approx 28 Metric Tons for Onions (40ft Reefer), 25 MT for Rice (20ft Dry), and 14-18 MT for Spices. For high-value commodities like Green Cardamom or Saffron, we accept smaller consolidated air cargo orders starting from 500 kg.
We operate under standard international trade finance instruments: 100% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC at Sight) issued by a prime international bank, or Telegraphic Transfer (TT) with a 30% advance deposit upon contract signing and 70% against faxed Bill of Lading copy.
All spices undergo calibrated hot-air drying or sun-curing with electronic hygrometer checks before bagging. Chillies are stored in temperature-controlled cold chains (+4°C) to prevent aflatoxin development and maintain color ASTA value throughout maritime transit.
Yes. We frequently arrange independent third-party inspections by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or Intertek at the port of stuffing. The surveyor inspects weight, sampling, pesticide analysis, and seals containers before customs release.
For ready-harvest inventory like Nashik Onions and Basmati Rice, stuffing and customs gating at JNPT or Mundra takes 3 to 5 business days from contract execution. Custom-branded packaging lots take 8 to 12 days for initial cylinder prep and printing.
