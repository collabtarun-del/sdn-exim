## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-prestige trade portal bridging traditional agricultural provenance with sovereign international commodity finance. The visual tone speaks to global enterprise buyers, sovereign wealth procurement officers, and Tier-1 import conglomerates. 

The aesthetic marries **High-End Heritage Editorial** with **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Editorial weight through authoritative serif headlines that project multi-generational stability, trust, and trade lineage.
- Polished modernism via crisp sans-serif metrics and structured, high-density transactional data displays.
- Translucent, frosted glass cards overlaid against layered warm ivory tones, accented by slender brushed-gold delineations and rich spice-market chroma.
- Ambient feelings of security, premium harvest terroir, and rigorous institutional logistics.
